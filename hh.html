Priyanka Goel has spent over 18 years holding space for people at every kind of crossroads — those seeking clarity, those carrying grief, those simply searching for a thread back to themselves.

Her work draws on four modalities: Reiki energy healing, Tarot reading, Vedic Numerology, and Akashic Records — each a different doorway into the same quiet truth. She offers both personal healing sessions and teaches these practices to those who feel called to carry them forward.

Based in India and available online worldwide, Priyanka's sessions are intimate, unhurried, and rooted in deep listening. Whether you come with a question or simply a feeling, there is space for you here.

The Approach

Healing as a coming home.

Every session at Healing Hands begins with one thing: listening. Not just to your words, but to the silences between them, the energy you carry in, the questions you haven't found words for yet.

Priyanka's work is never about fixing. It's about returning — to clarity, to ease, to the version of yourself that was always there.

You are not broken

Every person who walks in carries their own wholeness. Healing is not repair — it's the gentle removal of what was never truly yours to carry.

The body knows

Long before the mind finds words, the body speaks. Priyanka's practice honours that intelligence — working with energy, sensation, and presence rather than against them.

Transformation is quiet

Real change rarely announces itself loudly. It arrives as a small shift in how you hold your mornings, a decision made with unusual ease, a heaviness you realise is gone.

Every path is valid

Whether you come through Reiki, Tarot, Numerology, or the Akashic Records — there is no hierarchy of healing. The right door is the one that calls to you.
